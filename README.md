#  Blue Archive: The Silly Mobile Game Where Cute Girls have Halos... and Guns.

Blue Archive is a mobile RPG game developed by the Korean game development company Nexon, initialy released in 2021. In this game,
you take the role of the Sensei, a teacher who mysteriously ends up in Kivotos, an academy city inhabited by young female students
that carry guns and bear halos, which gives them the ability to withstand gunfire. Here, you will be in charge of a club called
SCHALE, which was founded to respond to different problems that pop up in the different academies across the whole of Kivotos.
From there, you will be recruiting and building up students, participate in various missions, and admire a beautifully crafted
story covered in dramatic moments and mysteries that surround Kivotos... oh, and also, there's the character designs. I'll leave it
at that.

As of the writing of this readme file, Blue Archive's Global release is about to celebrate its 4th anniversary, while the Japanese release, which is
a little older, is going to celebrate its 5th anniversary. I have actually been playing the Global release for about two and a half years,
although recently, I have been playing less because of a *certain game that I would be covering here should I get the go-ahead from
my instructor...*
